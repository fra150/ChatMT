ChatMT

Reflective and regenerative AI — Phase 2 prototype: the colour preference engine.

This version is a rule-based system with a persistent memory. It compares a colour you pick with the written standard codes, measures the perceptual distance, and remembers your choice on this device only. It does not train a neural network, it does not use a language model, and it sends nothing to any server.

    The measurement is a CIEDE2000 distance in CIELAB. It is not a statement about your taste: two colours can be very close to each other without you liking both.
